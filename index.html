A private, simple way to keep track of your gestational diabetes journey.

Gestational Diabetes Tracker is an Android app for recording and organising the health information you are asked to keep an eye on during a pregnancy affected by gestational diabetes.

Your health information is designed to stay on your device.

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. What you record is saved in a database on your phone, and it is only shared when you choose to export or share it yourself.

What the app helps you organise

  • Blood glucose readings — fasting, pre-meal, post-meal, bedtime and other checks
  • Meals, carbohydrate amounts and optional meal photos
  • Meal timers and glucose check reminders
  • Blood pressure, weight, ketones and medication or insulin doses
  • Pregnancy profile details, including due date and current week
  • Notes and symptom tags alongside your readings
  • Trends, time in range and patterns drawn from your own entries
  • PDF and CSV reports to bring to appointments

Built to work offline

Reminders are scheduled on the device itself, so they continue to work without a network connection. Because your information is stored locally, the app also includes backup and export tools so you can move your data to a new phone or keep a copy somewhere you control.

Medical disclaimer. This app is for tracking and informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical care, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult your healthcare professional for medical advice.
